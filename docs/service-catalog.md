# Parivahan Service Catalog

The seed catalog is maintained from the publicly listed Parivahan service groups:

- Driving licence and learner services
- Vehicle registration and post-registration services
- Permit, fitness, tax, and checkpost services
- Dealer and manufacturer services
- Digital services, informational services, and dashboards

Each catalog entry is classified as one of the following:

- `guided`: a workflow implemented and tracked in this application.
- `official_portal`: an authoritative handoff to the relevant Parivahan portal. These services can have state, RTO, vehicle-class, and eligibility-specific requirements, so this application does not present an unverified local form.

Source pages to review when refreshing the catalog:

- https://parivahan.gov.in/contactus
- https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/other-services-page
- https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/
- https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelectBean.do

The seed data uses only synthetic identities, vehicle registrations, documents, references, and case histories. Do not replace it with production personal data.
